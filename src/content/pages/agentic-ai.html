Controlled Architecture

How Our AI Agents Work

Backed by our end-to-end AI development services, every agent runs inside a deterministic control loop:

Understand Goal→Plan Steps→Act via Approved Tools→Verify Output→Ask a person when the stakes are high
Reasoning & Planning Engine

Permission-Scoped Tools (MCP)

Policy Guardrails & Approval Gates

Full Observability & Replay

Logs every step, tool call, and decision rationale for complete audit replay.

The controls built into every agent

Least-privilege tool access

Each agent gets only the tools and data scopes its task needs, tied to your identity provider.

Action thresholds

Limits on value, volume and type of action (e.g. refunds above [$500] always need approval).

Input and output guardrails

Checks for prompt injection, sensitive data, policy breaches and off-topic actions before anything executes.

Human-in-the-loop queues

Approvals and escalations arrive in tools your team already uses: Teams, Slack, email or your case system.

Full audit trail

Every plan, tool call, input, output and approval is logged and replayable, producing governance evidence for auditors.

Kill switch and rollback

Pause any agent instantly; reversible actions can be rolled back.

Questions buyers ask · Frequently Asked Questions

AI Agent Development Services: Common Questions

How is agentic AI different from generative AI and chatbots?

Generative AI creates content, and chatbots answer questions. Agentic AI solutions use reasoning to take action across your software, verifying eligibility, calling APIs, updating records, and escalating exceptions to humans.

How do you stop an AI agent from taking a wrong or harmful action?

Through layered controls: least-privilege tool access, dollar and volume thresholds, input/output guardrails, human-in-the-loop approval queues, graduated autonomy levels, and an instant kill switch.

What are MCP and A2A, and why do they matter?

Model Context Protocol (MCP) standardizes secure connections between agents and enterprise tools; Agent-to-Agent (A2A) lets specialist agents collaborate across frameworks. Both keep your agents portable and free from vendor lock-in.

Can AI agents work with legacy systems that lack modern APIs?

Yes. We connect via existing APIs, build lightweight MCP wrappers, or pair agents with RPA and computer-use automation for screen-based legacy apps; all under the same permissions and audit logs.

How long does it take to build and launch an AI agent?

A first production agent typically launches in 6 to 8 weeks through our fixed-scope Agent Pilot, including integration, readiness testing, and shadow-mode validation. Subsequent agents deploy faster on the same platform.

How do you measure accuracy and ROI?

We establish baseline KPIs (cost per case, cycle time, error rate) before build, benchmark against historical cases pre-launch, and track live task success, escalation rates, unit cost, and hours saved monthly.
