Before you talk to us

What leaders usually ask us first

Can you take over a pilot built by another vendor or our own team?

Yes. We review the code, data and architecture, keep what is production-ready and rebuild only what isn't.

How long does it take?

A typical path is about [12] weeks: review in weeks 1–2, plan in week 3, engineering and go-live after that. We confirm exact dates in the plan, based on what the review finds.

Do you work with our existing cloud and models?

Yes. Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, Anthropic, open-source models and on-premise.

How do you get security to sign off?

Access controls, PII masking, red-team tests and an evidence pack are built in, and shared with your security team early.

How do you measure success after go-live?

Metrics and a baseline are agreed before build, then tracked and reported monthly.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. Source code, prompts, configurations, test sets and documentation.
