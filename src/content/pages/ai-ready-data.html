Before you talk to us

What leaders usually ask us first

Do we have to move all our data to the cloud first?

No. We connect or move only what your first AI use case needs, and keep existing reports running.

Which platforms do you work with?

Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, Azure, AWS and Google Cloud. We build on what you already run.

Can you handle PDFs, emails and scanned documents?

Yes. We parse, classify and extract them into structured data, then test search quality on real questions.

Is our data used to train AI models?

Never. Your data stays in your environment, under your access rules, and is not shared with any AI model provider.

How long does it take?

A readiness score takes [2] weeks. The data layers for a first use case usually follow in weeks 3 to 10, about [8] weeks.

What happens after the readiness check?

You keep the scorecard and plan. We can build the layers with you, or hand the plan to your team.
