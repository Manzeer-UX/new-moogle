Before you talk to us

What leaders usually ask us first

Does the EU AI Act apply to us?

It can apply outside the EU if your AI is used in the EU market. Our assessment maps each system. This is general information, not legal advice.

How do you find shadow AI?

We review network, identity and expense data, and survey teams, then offer approved alternatives.

What is PRISM AI?

Our observability and evaluation platform. It traces every request, scores quality and safety, and keeps the audit trail.

Will governance slow our AI projects down?

Done well, it speeds them up: teams know the approval path from the start.

How do you protect against prompt injection?

Layered defences: input filtering, least-privilege tools, output checks, human approval for high-risk steps and red-team testing.

What evidence do you provide?

Inventory, risk assessment, lineage, documentation, test results, approvals and monitoring records, mapped to your frameworks.
