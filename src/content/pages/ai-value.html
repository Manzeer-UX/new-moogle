Before you talk to us

What leaders usually ask us first

How do you reduce AI costs without hurting quality?

Every change is tested against your quality scores before it ships. If quality drops, the change doesn't go live.

Which costs do you look at?

Model and API spend, cloud and GPU, tools, and the team time to run each system.

How do you measure value?

We link each system to a business metric, such as hours saved, revenue or errors avoided, agreed with you.

Do we need PRISM AI?

It makes tracking easier, but we can start from your billing and usage exports.

How long does it take?

The review takes [2–3] weeks. Quick wins often go live in the first month.

What happens after the review?

You keep the report and plan. We can apply the savings with you and report value monthly.
