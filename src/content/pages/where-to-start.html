Before you share your idea

What leaders usually ask us first

What kind of ideas can I share?

Anything. A process that eats time, a customer pain point, a competitor's move, or simply "we should be using AI". A rough idea is enough.

Do I need a fully defined requirement?

No. Most teams arrive with a goal, not a spec. Shaping the idea into a use case is part of the work.

Will you look at ideas or pilots we already have?

Yes. We score them alongside new ideas, so you can see what to keep, fix or stop.

What do you need from our side?

A sponsor, [2–3] hours from each team involved, and access to sample data or system documentation. We can work under your NDA.

How long does it take?

The first call is 45 minutes. A validated PoC usually takes 4 weeks, depending on data access and the systems involved.

What happens after the PoC?

You own everything. If the PoC passes, we can take it to production with you through our AI-to-Production service, or hand it to your team or another partner.
